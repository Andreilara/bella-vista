Place additional gallery photos in this folder. They appear after any legacy gallery photos and before the bundled concept images.
Name each file  <category>-<title>.jpg  -- the first word picks the filter tab:

  cover-  / hero-     -> Cover (home-page hero)  e.g. cover-front-view.jpg
  rooms-  / std-       -> Rooms          e.g. rooms-room-a-bed.jpg
  fam-    / family-    -> Family Rooms   e.g. family-fr2-living-area.jpg
  hall-                -> Event Hall     e.g. hall-wedding-setup.jpg
  events-              -> Events         e.g. events-corporate-night.jpg
  grounds- / garden-   -> Grounds        e.g. grounds-mountain-view.jpg
  dining-              -> Dining

Tips for sharp photos: upload the ORIGINAL JPG from the camera or phone, landscape, at least 1920px and ideally about 2400px on the long edge,
saved at JPG quality 80-85 (roughly 0.5-2 MB; the upload limit is 8 MB). Do not use photos saved from Facebook, Messenger or WhatsApp (they are shrunk and recompressed),
screenshots, or PNG for photographs. iPhone HEIC must be exported as JPG first. The website converts to AVIF/WebP and makes the small sizes by itself.
Frames are cropped to fit (wide, tall and square), so keep the main subject near the centre. Use squoosh.app to resize or compress.
Until photos are added, the site shows placeholder frames.
